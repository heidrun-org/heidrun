# Code of Conduct

In Valhalla, the warriors fight all day and feast all night, and then they are friends again the next morning. This project works the same way: disagree about the code as much as you want, and stay kind to the people.

## Our promise

We want everyone to feel welcome in this project, whatever their experience, background, identity, or country.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with beginners.
- Criticize the code, never the person.
- Accept feedback gracefully, and give it gently.
- Assume good intentions, and ask when something is not clear.

## What we do not accept

- Insults, threats, or harassment of any kind.
- Discriminatory jokes or comments. Jokes about goats and mead are fine. Jokes at the expense of a person are not.
- Publishing the private information of another person.
- Any other behavior that a reasonable person would find unwelcome in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all spaces of the project: issues, pull requests, discussions, and any place where a person speaks for the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainers can edit or remove comments, close issues, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, contact the maintainers [Eric Defiez](https://github.com/edefiez) and [Jérôme Étienne](https://github.com/jeromeetienne) through GitHub. Reports are kept private. The maintainers decide fairly, and nobody is thrown out of the hall without a reason.
