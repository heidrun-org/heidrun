Pin and unpin a pane ​

Keep a terminal visible while you switch tabs, then return the terminal to its own tab.

Find the pin button ​

A pane is one terminal inside a tab. Its pin button is in the pane header, beside the buttons for splitting and closing the pane.

The column of pinned panes appears at the right edge of the viewport, beside the panes of the selected tab. It is separate from the right sidebar.

Pin a pane ​

  1. Click the pin button in the pane header. The pane moves into the column of pinned panes.
  2. Select another tab. The pinned pane stays visible beside that tab’s panes, including when you select the tab that owns the pane.

Pinning changes where the pane is shown; the pane still belongs to its original tab.

Unpin a pane ​

  1. Click Unpin the pane in the pinned pane’s header.
  2. If another tab is selected, select the pane’s own tab to see the pane there again.

The pane returns to its own tab. When another tab is selected, the pane disappears from the column of pinned panes.

Troubleshooting ​

Another pane cannot be pinned ​

Cause: Heidrun allows at most four pinned panes.

Solution:

  1. Click Unpin the pane in a pinned pane’s header.
  2. Try pinning the other pane again.

Check: The other pane appears in the column of pinned panes.
