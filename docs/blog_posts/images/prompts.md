# Illustration prompts

- Created: 17 October 2026
- Last updated: 17 October 2026
- Status: Final prompts and provenance for generated illustrations
- Related framing: [Series plan](../blog_series_plan.md)
- Related rules: [Marketing images](../../marketing/images.md)
- Related issue: [issue #642](https://github.com/heidrun-org/heidrun/issues/642)

All three illustrations were generated with the built-in imagegen tool on 17 October 2026, with an opaque background, and saved as PNG files at 1536 by 1024 pixels. No input reference images were used. The outputs were visually inspected, copied into this folder, and used without image edits. The illustrations are editorial artwork rather than product screenshots or measured charts.

The final prompts sent to the tool follow.

## three-features.png

```text
Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: polished editorial cover for a technical blog post about Heidrun, a desktop workspace for directing coding agents. Primary request: show one developer at a desk calmly overseeing three distinct ongoing software tasks, with three luminous terminal-like windows at different stages and one small amber attention signal. Wide landscape 1536x1024. Sophisticated ink and gouache editorial illustration, deep navy, warm ivory, restrained amber and teal, visible paper texture, strong architectural composition, human scale, thoughtful and credible. Windows are abstract shapes, no legible interface, no text, no logos, no robots, no invented product screenshot. Concept: keeping track of three features and knowing which coding agent needs attention. Compose so image works as a social preview.
```

## development-history.png

```text
Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: editorial blog cover for the second article in a Heidrun series. Primary request: a developer tracing a single glowing thread through layered archival sheets: a code page, a conversation, an issue record, a pull request record, revealing the original decision behind software. Wide landscape 1536x1024. Sophisticated ink and gouache editorial illustration, deep navy, warm ivory, restrained amber and teal, visible paper texture; the composition is readable and quiet, physical sheets connected through depth, one continuous line. No legible text, no logos, no robots, no invented interface or screenshots. Concept: recovering the why from development history. Match a coherent technical publication series.
```

## day-of-development.png

```text
Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: editorial blog cover for the third article in a Heidrun series. Primary request: a developer studying the traces of a day's software work laid out across a desk: an abstract timeline of activity, several commit-like marks, a token ledger and modest bar chart, alongside notes from conversations. Wide landscape 1536x1024. Sophisticated ink and gouache editorial illustration, deep navy, warm ivory, restrained amber and teal, visible paper texture, elegant geometric composition. No numerical labels, no text, no logos, no robots, no exaggerated upward growth arrow, no product screenshot. Concept: using evidence to understand a working day rather than claiming that more activity proves better software. Match a coherent technical publication series.
```
