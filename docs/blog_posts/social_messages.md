## Three Features in Progress. Which One Needs Me?

I started a second feature while a coding agent worked on the first. Then I lost track of which change needed me. That is why I built Heidrun: one workspace for coding agents, their questions, and the work ready to review. I am looking for early users and feedback on real workflows.

## What Does a Day of Development With Coding Agents Actually Look Like?

A measured week in Heidrun: 235 pull requests opened and 288 issues opened by my GitHub account. What do those numbers explain? I examined the history, token usage, estimated cost, and counting rules. The result is a way to ask better questions about a workflow. I would like early users to try their own.
