This document gives one short definition for each word of the status bar and the information card. The other words of Heidrun are in [index.md](index.md).

## The status bar

### Status bar

The horizontal row at the bottom of the window. It holds status bar sections.

### Status bar section

One part of the status bar. It shows one piece of information, for example the usage of Claude, the usage of Codex, or the Git branch. A click on a status bar section opens its information card.

### Information card

A small card that opens on a click, never when the mouse pointer only moves over its part. It gives more detail about that part, and it can hold buttons. It opens above a status bar section, at the left of a part of the right sidebar, at the right of a part of the left sidebar, or below a circled question mark of a page of the Settings window. The part gets a background while the pointer is over it and while the information card is open. A second click on the part, the key Escape, a click outside, and the loss of the keyboard focus close the information card. The files line of a chat in the Past chats sidebar section has the first information card that opens at the left of a part of a sidebar.

### Workspace status bar section

The first status bar section, at the left end of the status bar. It shows the status dot and the name of the selected workspace. Its information card gives the details of the selected workspace, and lists the workspaces to select, rename, close, move, or create.
