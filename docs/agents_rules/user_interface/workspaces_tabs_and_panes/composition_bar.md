## Composition bar

- A drop of image files on the window, on any part of the window, opens the composition bar and adds the images to it. A file that is not an image gets the message "This file is not an image". The same file is never added twice.
- The composition bar sits under the terminal of the focused pane, inside the border of the pane. It exists only after a drop or after a click on the toggle button of the header of the pane, and until the developer sends it or hides it, so a pane that never received a drop has its whole height. The composition bar follows the focus: it is for the focused pane only.
- When the composition bar has images, its first line holds one small picture for each image, and nothing else. With no image, this line does not exist, and the composition bar has one line. Each picture is 144 by 96 pixels, and shows a large image icon while its preview loads. Each picture has a round-cornered button of 30 pixels in its top right corner that removes it. Removing the last image keeps the composition bar open and removes the first line. The composition bar shows no text that counts the images.
- The last line, which is the only line when there is no image, holds from left to right: the plus button, the text input, the push-to-talk button (only when the voice input is on), and the button "Send". The plus button is on this line, so it never moves when an image is added or removed. It opens the file picker of the system, which accepts several image files.
- The composition bar has air: 14 pixels of space above and below, 16 pixels at each side, 16 pixels between two lines, and 4 pixels between two controls of the last line. The last line is the exception at the sides: it starts and ends 8 pixels from the edge of the bar, half of the 16 pixels, so that the icon of the plus button lines up with the left edge of the pictures. The controls of the last line are 46 pixels high, and the text of the text input is 20% bigger than the other text of the window. The buttons of this line are icon buttons, 46 pixels high, with a width that leaves 6.3 pixels at each side of the icon, with no text, no border, and no background at rest: only the icon shows, and a background shows under the pointer: white at 14% strength in the dark theme, and black at 14% strength in the light theme, through the variable `--wash`. Each has a label for the screen readers, and the button "Send" has a title that names its key, Enter.
- The text input takes several lines. The text of a recording goes at its end. It has a label for the screen readers and a placeholder, and it grows with its lines up to 160 pixels, then scrolls. An empty text input is one line high, whatever the length of the placeholder. The key Enter sends, and the keys Shift and Enter write a new line. While the microphone is open, the level bars and the elapsed time are inside the text input at its right end, as [settings_voice_input.md](../window/settings_voice_input.md) says.
- When an image arrives, the text input gets the keyboard. The key Enter in the text input (without Shift), and the button "Send", paste each image in the focused pane one after the other, with a wait of half a second. When the text is not empty, Heidrun then writes the text in the pane and presses Enter in the pane. When the text is empty, Heidrun writes nothing and presses nothing, so the developer keeps the keyboard in the terminal. Then the composition bar closes and the keyboard goes back to the terminal.
- When the microphone is open, or Whisper still changes the speech into text, the key Enter and the button "Send" first stop the recording, wait for the text, and add it to the text input. Only then do they send the images and the whole text. The button "Send" is dimmed during this wait. When the recording or the change into text fails, nothing is sent, the composition bar stays open, and the line under the text input tells why.
- When a paste fails, the images that were not pasted and the text stay in the composition bar.
- The toggle button of the header of the focused pane shows and hides the composition bar. It is the first of the tool buttons of the header, before the macro menu, and only the focused pane has it, whether the voice input is on or off. Its icon is a text input with a cursor (`input-cursor-text`), it has a label and a title, and it is pressed and orange while the composition bar is open, like the pressed pin button. One click opens the composition bar with no image, and the next click hides it. The composition bar has no button "Close".
- Hiding the composition bar, with the toggle button or with the key Escape, removes every image and the text, throws away a recording of the voice input that runs, and closes the composition bar.
- A click on a picture opens the big view. The big view shows one large picture, with its file name and its position (for example "2 of 3"), a button on each side, and a close button. The key Left and the key Right, and the two buttons, go to the previous and to the next image, and the last image is followed by the first image. The key Escape, the close button, and a click beside the picture close the big view. The big view takes its keys before the terminal and the composition bar.
- A drop never pastes at once, and never pastes in the pane under the pointer.
- The voice input ([issue 662](https://github.com/heidrun-org/heidrun/issues/662)): with the switch "Enable voice input" on, a line under the text input tells why a recording failed. The rules of the push-to-talk button are in [settings_voice_input.md](../window/settings_voice_input.md).
