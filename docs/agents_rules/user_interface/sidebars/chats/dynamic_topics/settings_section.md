# Past chats sidebar section: the section "Dynamic topics" of the Settings window

- Created: 13 October 2026
- Last updated: 14 October 2026
- Status: Maintained rules for agents and tools
- Related framing: [Project instructions](../../../../../../AGENTS.md)
- Related rules: [Glossary of Heidrun](../../../../glossary/index.md), [Glossary of Heidrun: dynamic topics](../../../../glossary/dynamic_topics.md)

This file is a part of the rules of the user interface of Heidrun. The words are defined in [glossary/index.md](../../../../glossary/index.md) and [glossary/dynamic_topics.md](../../../../glossary/dynamic_topics.md).

- The Settings window has a section "Dynamic topics", the last section of its sidebar (icon `tags`). The section holds the choice "Extraction method": a Bootstrap 6 button group of two radio inputs (`btn-group` with `btn-check btn-outline theme-secondary` labels), "Keyword" and "Language model". The choice is the option `dynamicTopicExtraction` of the settings, and the default is "keyword". Both radio inputs are enabled. The title of the button "Language model" says that the coding agent `codex` or `claude` that is installed on this computer finds the topics. The more menu has no entry for it.
- The title "Extraction method" is followed, on the same line, by a circled question mark (the icon `question-circle`, in muted color). The section has no help paragraph under the button group: the circled question mark holds the help. A click on it opens an information card below it, with a title (the question mark icon and the text "Extraction method"), then two blocks: "Keyword" says that the topics of each chat come from the words of its title, of the first messages that the developer typed, of the folders of the files that the coding agent wrote, and of the titles of its issues and pull requests, and that nothing leaves this computer; "Language model" says that a language model finds the topics, and that Heidrun sends the title and the first messages that the developer typed, of the chats that the list shows, and nothing else, to the coding agent `codex` or `claude` that is installed on this computer. Each block has its name in the medium weight, and its text in muted color.
- The information card of a circled question mark in the Settings window follows the rules of every information card: it opens on a click, and a second click on the circled question mark, the key Escape, a click outside, and the loss of the keyboard focus close it. It opens below the circled question mark, with its arrow under the middle of the circled question mark, and it stays inside the window. The component `InformationCard` has the property `placement` for this: "start" (the default) opens the card at the left of its part, and "bottom" opens it below.
